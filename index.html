Frequently Asked Questions

Microblading typically takes about 2-2.5 hours from start to finish, including consultation, mapping, and the procedure itself. Lip blush takes slightly longer, about 2.5-3 hours. The combo package usually takes about 4-4.5 hours with a short break between procedures.

Most clients report minimal discomfort. We use a topical anesthetic to numb the area before and during the procedure. Pain tolerance varies by individual, but most describe it as a light scratching sensation. The lips tend to be more sensitive than the brows.

The healing process takes about 4-6 weeks. Initially, the color will appear darker and more intense, then it will lighten as the skin heals. There may be some flaking or scabbing. It's important to follow all aftercare instructions carefully. Final results are visible after the touch-up appointment.

Microblading typically requires a touch-up every 12-18 months to maintain optimal results. Lip blush lasts longer, usually 2-3 years before needing a refresh. Factors like skin type, sun exposure, and lifestyle can affect how long the pigment lasts. We recommend an annual color boost for both services.

You should avoid these procedures if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes (uncontrolled), are undergoing chemotherapy, have skin diseases or irritations in the treatment area, or are prone to keloid scarring. We also don't recommend it for those with very oily skin as it may affect microblading results.
